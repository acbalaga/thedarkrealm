# Lore bible

Canon, voice, and the text the tickets need. Text in this file is final copy unless a ticket says otherwise. Add it to `data.js` verbatim.

## 1. Canon

Established by the shipped Codex. Do not contradict.

- **Vesperdeep** is where shadows sink when the living die. It is built from Gloom, which is what dark becomes when left alone.
- **The player** is a shadow that woke without an owner and sat in an empty throne. The denizens call the player **Regent**: someone who keeps a chair warm. They bow to the seat, not to the player.
- **Ysmerel, the Unseen** was the queen. She performed the first Unlighting alone, and the pressure made her the first Nightglass. Every shard is a piece of the same pane.
- **Nightglass** reflects what stood in front of it before. In it, the seat is not empty.
- **The Auric Concord** is a government of lamplighters above. They cut Sunshafts into the realm to render shadow into tallow for lamps. They are drilling for fuel. They are increasingly afraid.
- **Echoes** are scraps of the dead carried down by their shadows: a habit, a tune, how they held a cup.
- **The Hush-Wardens** predate the throne room. They serve the quiet.
- **The Sunless Sea** lies under the realm. **The Sleeper Below** is at its bottom and breathes once an age.
- **The First Lamp** is the first lamp the Concord lit, on a kitchen table, burning tallow rendered from a shadow named on the Ledger's oldest page. The Host carried it down. Now the seat casts a shadow.

Open questions the plan answers: whose shadow the player is (the Pane, section 9), and who dug before the Concord (Elder Shafts, section 10).

## 2. Voice

- Dry, plain, a little funny. The narrator reports and does not react.
- Short declarative sentences. One long sentence per paragraph at most, to carry an image.
- Concrete nouns: a cup, a hook, a chain, a kitchen table. No abstractions about destiny or power.
- The Concord's own words are bureaucratic and nervous. Reports, audits, recommendations.
- The denizens are literal-minded and never explain themselves.
- No exclamation marks. No em dashes. No second-person commands outside button labels. No modern idiom, no jokes that wink at games.
- Menace comes from understatement. "Do not ask what else it dreams."
- Button labels are two or three words and name the act: "Let go", "Take the seat", "Lower the line".

## 4. New Codex pages

Each has an id, a category (see TDR-040), an unlock, and text.

### The Sky

**surfaceclock** · The Surface Clock · when the Surface Clock unlocks
> The Scribes have worked out the hours above from the lanterns. When the lamps come thick it is the change of shift. When the shafts go quiet the surface is asleep, and the dark runs down the walls with nothing to stop it. You have started keeping their hours. It is the only thing you have in common.

**moonless** · The Moonless Night · first Moonless Night seen
> Once in every turn of the moon there is a night with nothing in the sky. The Concord doubles its watch. The Cantors hold one note until morning. Nightglass cut on that night comes out thicker, and nobody will say why.

**lidless** · The Lidless Moon · first full moon seen
> A full moon is a lamp nobody lit. The Lampwardens work by it without tallow and count that as thrift. Their shafts stay crewed all night, which means more shadows on the chains, which means more Echoes for you. The Concord has not noticed the arithmetic.

**longestnight** · The Longest Night · first winter solstice seen
> The denizens keep one holiday. Nobody announces it. The Gleaners put down their hooks, the Forge goes quiet, and the whole realm listens to the surface run out of day. Then everyone goes back to work, and the work goes better than it has all year.

**longestday** · The Longest Day · first summer solstice seen
> Up there they light bonfires and call it a festival. Down here it is the day the chains run fastest. The Scribes mark it in the Ledger with a small drawing of a sun, crossed out.

**balance** · The Balance · first equinox seen
> Twice a year the day and the night are the same length and neither side will admit it. The Wardens stand in the doorways. Nothing happens. The Wardens consider this a success.

**performance** · The Performance · first eclipse witnessed
> Forty voices, rehearsing since before the throne had a room. Today the sun was covered and they sang it through once, start to end, with no mistakes. Afterward the conductor put down the baton and asked when the next one was. The Scribes knew.

### The Court

**petitions** · Petitions · first petition granted
> The denizens do not ask for things. They inform the seat of what they intend and wait to see if it objects. The Scribes call these petitions, to be polite. You may grant one a day. They have noticed which ones you choose.

**litanies** · Litanies · first litany spoken
> A rite is slow. A litany is the same thing said fast, by everyone, at once. The Cantors can manage it for a few breaths and need a long silence afterward. The Lampwardens have a word for it in their reports. The word is "weather".

**vigil** · The Vigil · 7 vigils kept
> The Scribes have started a tally of the days you came to the seat and granted something. They do not count the days you did not. When asked why, the eldest said that a ledger of absences is just a list of everything.

### The Sea

**soundings** · Soundings · first sounding returned
> The Tidewells go down further than their ropes. Someone tied on more rope. What came up was not Gloom. It was a cup, whole, with the glaze worn where a thumb had rested for forty years. The Scribes made a new page.

**set_table** · The Drowned Table · set complete
> Four things from one table. Laid out together they make a place setting for someone who ate alone and did not mind. The Echoes come more easily now, as if they had somewhere to sit.

**set_survey** · The Surveyor's Kit · set complete
> Prospector-General Askerel's instruments, dropped down a shaft on purpose. The level has no bubble. The Host has learned to read it anyway. It says the same thing in every direction, which is down.

**set_loft** · The Choir Loft · set complete
> The fork hums at dusk without being struck. The page is all rests. The Cantors say it is the most difficult piece they own and they have never once got through it without somebody breathing.

**set_lamp** · The Lamp Room · set complete
> A wick never lit, a snuffer, a tin, a lens. Somebody up there kept a lamp room and never used it. The Scribes think it was a lamplighter who preferred the dark. They have given the wick its own shelf.

**set_queen** · The Queen's Things · set complete
> A comb. A ring too large for any hand. A key to no door in the realm. A glazier's diamond, for cutting panes. The Cantors would not touch any of them. The Weavers asked, carefully, whether you wanted the diamond sharpened.

**set_bed** · The Sleeper's Bed · set complete
> You have been taking things from under something that is asleep. It has not woken. The Wardens stand a little further off than they did. The breath in the jar has not been opened and will not be.

### The Deep

**currents** · Currents · first current chosen
> The realm does not sink straight down. There are currents in the deep dark, and each fathom has a grain to it, the way wood does. The Wardens can feel three of them from the seat. They will tell you which is which. They will not tell you which to take.

**vows** · Vows · Vows unlock
> The oldest Hush-Warden kept a vow for an age and will not say what it was. It says only that a thing given up on the way down is waiting, heavier, at the bottom. The Scribes have a column for vows. It was already ruled when they opened the page.

**pane** · The Pane · first Nightglass (replaces nothing, sits after "Nightglass")
> Lay the shards out on the floor of the throne room and they do not fit each other. They fit a shape that is not here yet. The Weavers have drawn it: a round window, twenty-four lights, leaded in black. One light is already filled. It is the one you were holding when you woke.

**pane_half** · Twelve Lights · 12 lights lit
> Half a window. Through it the throne room looks the way it did, which is to say, occupied. She is not sitting. She is standing at the pane with one hand flat on the other side, the way a person stands at a door they have decided not to knock on.

**elder** · The Elder Shafts · seal Sunshaft 151
> Past the First Lamp the shafts do not stop. These were not cut by the Concord. The stone is older and the chains are a metal the Forge cannot name. Someone dug for shadow before there were lamplighters. Toward the end, they also used longer chains.

**bell** · The Drowned Bell · first Drowned Bell bound
> It was cast to call people up out of a mine. It rang once too late and was thrown down the shaft after them. It still rings. Nothing comes up. The Gleaners find this restful.

**root** · What Is Under the Seat · seal Sunshaft 200
> At the top of the oldest shaft there was no lamp. There was a hole in a field, grown over, and a stone with a name worn off it. Your Host stood in daylight for the first time and cast nothing. It came home and reported that the surface is thin, and that most of the world is down here, and always was.

## 9. The Pane and the endings (TDR-035)

The reveal: the Regent is Ysmerel's own shadow, left on the floor when she became glass. That is why it woke with no owner, and why the denizens bow to the seat and look from the seat to the player.

**Modal when the 24th light is lit: "The Pane, Whole"**
> The last light goes in and the window is a window.
>
> She is on the other side of it, and she is not looking at the room. She is looking at you, the way a person looks at their own hand.
>
> Every shadow belongs to something. You did not know whose you were because she was not there to be cast by. She is there now. The First Lamp is burning at the foot of the throne, and for the first time in an age, there is a light behind her.
>
> The Cantors have stopped. The denizens are looking at the seat, and then at the glass, and then at you.

Buttons: **Give her the seat** · **Keep the chair**. Each asks once: "This cannot be taken back."

**Ending A: The Seat, Returned** (codex id `end_returned`)
> You stood up. It was easier than you expected. The pane opened like a door that had only ever been closed from one side, and she sat, and the First Lamp threw her shadow across the floor of the throne room, long and patient, and you were in it. You were it. The denizens bowed. This time it was not to the chair.

Subtitle under the title becomes "The Queen's Shadow".

**Ending B: The Seat, Kept** (codex id `end_kept`)
> You stayed where you were. She watched you decide. Then she nodded, once, the way a person nods at their own reflection when it has finally done something on its own, and the pane went dark from her side. The denizens waited a while. Then the first Gleaner bowed, and it was not to the chair, and it was not to her.

Subtitle becomes "The Second Queen".

Both endings keep every bonus. The difference is the title, the scene tint (TDR-053) and the final page.
